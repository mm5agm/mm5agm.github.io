Run your transceiver from any browser

Yaesu Web Control and Icom Web Control put your radio on your PC, tablet or phone: a live spectrum, meters, CW and RTTY decoding, DX spots and more. Both are free and open source.

Yaesu Web Control

YWC

Browser-based control for Yaesu HF transceivers. Use the Classic page, the dockable Experimental UI, or the Radio panel, which is laid out like the front of the radio with working keys, knobs and main dial. As well as the Windows installer, there are macOS, Linux and Docker versions of YWC, which also run on a Raspberry Pi.

SDR spectrum and waterfall

Live panadapter from an SDRplay or other SDR, or the radio's own scope.

The radio's screen in your browser

The radio's own TFT screen, captured from its DVI socket, and it's clickable. Click a signal on the scope or waterfall and the radio tunes straight to it. Tested on the FTdx101MP/D and FTdx10. The FT-710 also has a DVI output, but this hasn't been tested.

CW and RTTY

CW reader and sender, an RTTY tuning scope with decode, and Auto set-up from the signal.

DX spots and watch list

DX cluster spots on the spectrum, and a popup and beep when a callsign or prefix you're watching appears.

Voice control

Hold the mic button and say what you want: band, mode, frequency and more. Recognition runs locally on your PC, with no cloud service. On the Classic page, Windows only.

Voice announcements

YWC can speak band, mode and TX changes, DX alerts and TX timeout warnings, so you know what the radio is doing without looking at the screen. On the Classic page.

Accessible

Full screen reader support for NVDA and Windows Narrator, High Contrast mode, and labels you can rename in any language. On the Classic page.

TX timeout warning

A red banner and tone if you've been transmitting too long, to catch an open mic or a stuck PTT.

Memories

Memory banks with ADIF import.

Works with your logger

Integrates with WSJT-X, Log4OM, JTAlert and GridTracker.

Supported radios

FTdx101MP FTdx101D FTdx10 FT-710 FTDX3000

Being tested: FT-991A, FTDX5000MP and FTDX5000D.

Runs on

Windows macOS Linux Docker / Raspberry Pi

The SDR spectrum display is Windows only. On macOS, Linux and Docker you can use the radio's own screen instead, captured from its DVI socket. These versions are the work of Fabio Valente, CR7CDC.

Icom Web Control

IWC

The companion to YWC for Icom CI-V transceivers. Frequency and mode, meters, PTT, the RX DSP, the CI-V spectrum scope, ATU and voice control, all from the browser.

CI-V spectrum scope

The radio's own scope and waterfall, streamed to the browser.

RTTY that decodes

Crossed-ellipse tuning scope and text decode. Auto measures shift and speed for you.

CW reader and sender

Copy and send Morse from the browser.

Voice control

Change band, mode and frequency by speaking.

WSJT-X bridge

Built-in rigctld bridge, so WSJT-X and IWC share the radio.

Three layouts

Classic, the Experimental UI and the Radio panel.

Supported radios

IC-7300 IC-7300 MkII

About

I'm Colin, MM5AGM, in Scotland. I write YWC and IWC for my own station, an FTdx101MP with an SDRplay for the spectrum and an IC-7300 MkII on the bench, and share them so other operators can use them too.

Thanks to Fabio Valente, CR7CDC, for the radio-screen capture, the Experimental UI, and the macOS, Linux and Docker versions of YWC, including running it on a Raspberry Pi.

If your radio, mode or setup is different from mine, I'd like to hear how you get on. Even a one-line "works fine" helps. Post on GitHub Discussions or open an issue on either project.
